# Arabic Language

Arabic is one of the most widely spoken languages in the world. It is the official language of many countries in the Middle East and North Africa.

## Welcome

This website is a friendly introduction to Arabic for learners and visitors who want to explore the language, script, and culture.

- Learn why Arabic is special
- Discover useful phrases
- Find help and resources on the Contact page

## Why Arabic is Special

- It has a rich history and strong literary traditions.
- It is the language of the Quran and many classical works.
- It is written from right to left, with beautiful flowing script.
- Arabic is spoken across many countries and has diverse dialects.

## Fun Facts

- Arabic uses the Arabic alphabet, which has 28 letters.
- There are many dialects, while Modern Standard Arabic is used in news, books, and formal writing.
- Learning Arabic opens doors to culture, history, and travel.

## Useful Phrases

- Marhaba مرحبا — Hello
- Assalam Alaykum السلام عليكم — Peace be upon you
- Shukran شكراً — Thank you
- Min fadlak من فضلك — Please
